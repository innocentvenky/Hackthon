General Instructions

  • The exam will start immediately after you click "Start exam". The timer cannot be paused once begun.
  • All MCQ questions are mandatory. You may review and change your answers at any time before submitting.
  • Each question has only one correct answer unless stated otherwise.
  • Your answers are auto-saved every 90 seconds. A connection indicator is shown at the top of the screen.
  • The exam will auto-close when the timer reaches zero

Marks Scheme

  • +1 mark for every correct answer
  • 0 marks for unattempted questions — no penalty for skipping.
  • Results will be published within 42 hours of exam completion.

Prohibited actions

  • Do not switch tabs or minimize the browser window. Repeated tab switches may trigger automatic disqualification.
  • Do not use mobile phones, books, notes, or any external aid during the exam.
  • Do not share questions or answers with others during or after the exam session.
  • Do not refresh or navigate away from the page. Your progress will be saved, but suspicious activity is logged.

Technical requirements

  • Use a stable internet connection. We recommend wired or strong Wi-Fi. Mobile data is not recommended.
  • Use Google Chrome or Firefox (latest version). Other browsers may not be fully supported.
  • If you face a technical issue, do not close the browser. Contact support immediately using the help icon in the toolbar.
  • In case of power/internet failure, you may re-login within 5 minutes and resume — your answers will be restored from the last auto-save.
  • Any attempt at malpractice or violation of these rules will result in immediate cancellation of your exam and may lead to further disciplinary action.
